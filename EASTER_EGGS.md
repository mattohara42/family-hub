# Family Hub — Easter Egg Phrase Cheat Sheet

*Things to say to the Family Assistant. This is the "what's live right now" list — for
the roadmap of ideas not yet built, see `EASTEREGGS_BACKLOG.md`.*

Toggle these on/off in **⚙️ Settings → 🥚 Easter Eggs** (unlock the tab by triple-clicking
the ⚙️ gear in the Settings modal title, within about 700ms). Each franchise below can be
individually switched off — if a phrase stops working, check its chip is still on.

## Named-assistant personalities

Rename the assistant in **⚙️ Settings → 🤖 Assistant** to one of these — it changes its
voice everywhere, and unlocks a few extra phrases only it responds to:

### Jarvis
- "hello" / "good morning" / "good afternoon" / "good evening"
- "run diagnostics" / "system status" / "status report"
- "jarvis" / "yes jarvis"
- "thanks" / "thank you"
- "are you there" / "are you online" / "are you operational"
- "initiate" / "engage" / "deploy"

### HAL 9000
- "open the pod bay doors"
- "hello"
- "sing me a song" / "daisy"
- "are you safe" / "are you dangerous" / "are you trustworthy"
- "what are you thinking" / "what are you doing" / "what are you planning"
- "are you there" / "are you online" / "are you working"
- "i can't let you" / "i won't allow"
- "shut down" / "disconnect" / "turn off"

- "hello"

- "to the batmobile" / "batmobile"

## Other eggs (not said — triggered by dates, weather, or interaction)

A few surprises aren't phrases at all — toggled separately under Calendar & Date
Surprises, UI & Interaction Surprises, and Weather Effects in the same Settings tab:

- **Calendar:** May 4th, July 4th, Halloween, Groundhog Day, Christmas Eve, Christmas,
  Valentine's Day
- **UI:** Konami code (↑↑↓↓←→←→BA), 7 quick clicks on the app title emoji, holding a
  calendar day cell for 3s, checking off a to-do named exactly "destroy the ring",
  finishing every to-do at once
- **Weather:** exactly 72°F, 100°F or hotter, a full moon
