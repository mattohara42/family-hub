      ⚙️ Settings

      Identity
      Theme
      A palette colors family members in list order and gives calendars, holidays and recurring items softer matching colors. Shared with every device.
      Layout & display
      📺 TV / Display mode
      Hides controls and enlarges text for casting. Add ?tv=1 to start in it, Escape exits.
      🖼 Wall display
      For a touchscreen read from across the room: evens out text sizes and keeps event text white. This device only.
      ⌨️ On-screen keyboard
      A touch keyboard for wall displays with no physical one.
      Motion & effects (this device only)
      Animations that never stop, and the frosted-glass blur. Each makes the screen work harder — switch them off on a slow wall panel.
      Background Images
      Requires Google sign-in · Enable the Photos Picker API in Google Cloud Console
      🖼 Or click to upload local images / drag & drop
      Screensaver
      🌙 Screensaver
      After a period of inactivity, fades to a full-screen ambient display. Touch the screen or press any key to wake.
      🔊 Pond sound
      Let the pond play its ambient chimes. Turn off for a silent pond.
      🕐 Show clock
      Large time & date overlay; drifts position slightly to protect the display from burn-in.
      🌤 Weather & next event
      Show the current temperature and the next calendar event under the clock.
      🌒 Night dimming
      During night hours the screensaver shows only a dim clock — no photos, no pond.

      Shared by Shopping, To-Do, Chores and Calendar. A phone number lets you text them the shopping list.
